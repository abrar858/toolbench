    Case converter

    Change text to upper case, lower case, title case or sentence case, or to the naming styles programmers use, in one click.

    • Free, no sign-up
    • Runs privately in your browser
    • Instant results

    How to use the case converter

    1. Type or paste your text.
    2. Click the case you want.
    3. Copy the result. Use Undo to go back a step.

    Which case should I use?

    • Sentence case: capital only at the start of each sentence. Good for body text and many headings.
    • Title Case: a capital at the start of every word, for titles and names.
    • camelCase and PascalCase: words joined together, common for JavaScript variables and class names.
    • snake_case and CONSTANT_CASE: words joined with underscores, common in Python and for constants.
    • kebab-case: words joined with hyphens, used in web addresses and CSS class names.

    Fixing caps lock text

    Typed a paragraph with caps lock on? Paste it and choose Sentence case or Toggle case to fix it without retyping.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I change text from capitals to lower case?

    Paste the text and click 'lower case', or 'Sentence case' to keep a capital at the start of each sentence.

    What is the difference between camelCase and PascalCase?

    camelCase starts with a lower-case letter (myVariableName). PascalCase starts with a capital (MyVariableName).

    Does Title Case follow style guides?

    It capitalises every word. Some style guides keep short words such as 'and' or 'of' in lower case, so check a title before publishing.
