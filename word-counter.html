Count words and characters instantly

Paste or type text into the word counter to see word count, characters, paragraphs, reading time and speaking time.

If the embedded tool does not load, open the calculator directly.

Worked example and use cases

Use the word counter to check the length of an assignment, article, CV, social post or speech. The reading and speaking times are estimates based on selectable speeds.

Important limitations

Different word processors can count hyphenated words, emoji and punctuation differently. Check the rules of your institution or publishing platform when an exact word limit matters.
