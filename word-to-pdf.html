    Word to PDF converter

    Turn a Word document (.docx) into a PDF without installing anything or uploading the file.

    • Free, no sign-up
    • Runs privately in your browser
    • Instant results
    ⇪Choose a Word file (.docx)or drag and drop a file here · files stay on your device

    Choose a .docx file to preview it.

    In the print window, choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer.

    How to convert Word to PDF

    1. Choose a .docx file.
    2. Check the preview of your document.
    3. Press Save as PDF. In the print window, choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer, then save.

    How the conversion works

    The document is read in your browser with the open-source mammoth library, which converts headings, paragraphs, bold and italic text, lists, tables, links and images. Your browser's built-in PDF printer then creates the PDF, so the text stays selectable and searchable.

    Limits

    Complex layouts such as text boxes, columns, headers and footers, tracked changes and exact fonts may not match Word exactly. For a pixel-perfect copy, use File → Save as → PDF in Word itself. Older .doc files need to be saved as .docx first.

    Print settings

    In the print window you can choose the paper size, turn off the browser's own headers and footers, and set margins. A4 with default margins suits most documents.

    Why send a PDF instead of a Word file

    A PDF looks the same on every device and can't be changed by accident, which is why employers, landlords and official forms usually ask for one. Fonts, spacing and page breaks in a Word file can shift on someone else's computer; a PDF keeps them fixed.

    On a phone

    • Android (Chrome): press Save as PDF, then choose Save as PDF as the printer and tap the download icon.
    • iPhone and iPad (Safari): press Save as PDF, then in the print preview tap the Share button and choose Save to Files.

    Getting a clean PDF

    • In Chrome's print window, open More settings and untick Headers and footers to remove the date and web address the browser adds.
    • Choose the paper size your recipient expects: A4 in the UK, Letter in the US.
    • If the document uses an unusual font that isn't installed on your device, a similar font is used instead. For an exact match, export from Word itself.

    What carries over from Word

    Headings, paragraphs, bold, italic and underlined text, bulleted and numbered lists, tables, images and links all appear in the PDF. Footnotes and endnotes are placed at the end of the document. Text boxes, shapes, page headers and footers, comments and exact page breaks aren't carried over, so for a CV or letter with a designed layout, use Word's own Save as PDF if you can.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert Word to PDF for free?

    Choose your .docx file, press Save as PDF and pick Save as PDF as the printer.

    Does it work on a phone?

    On iPhone and Android, the share sheet or print menu offers saving as PDF. The steps vary slightly by browser.

    Can I convert .doc files?

    Not directly. Open them in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice and save as .docx first.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Your files are processed by your own browser and never leave your device. Nothing is sent to a server, so it works for confidential documents and you can even use it offline once the page has loaded.

    Why does my PDF show the date and a web address?

    Your browser adds them. In the print window, open More settings and untick Headers and footers.
