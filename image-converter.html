    How to convert an image

    1. Choose one or more images, or drag them onto the box.
    2. Pick the format to convert to: JPG, PNG or WebP.
    3. For JPG and WebP, set the quality. 90% looks identical to the original for most photos.
    4. Press Convert, then download each image or all of them as a ZIP.

    Which format should I use?

    • JPG: the most compatible choice for photos. Doesn't support transparency, so transparent areas become white.
    • PNG: lossless and supports transparency. Best for logos, screenshots and graphics with text.
    • WebP: modern format with much smaller files than JPG or PNG, supported by all current browsers. Ideal for websites.

    Common conversions

    Convert WebP images saved from websites to JPG so they open everywhere, PNG screenshots to JPG to make them smaller, or JPG photos to WebP to speed up your website. Phone photos are turned the right way up automatically.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert WebP to JPG?

    Choose the WebP images, select JPG and press Convert.

    How do I convert PNG to JPG?

    Choose the PNG files, select JPG and press Convert. Transparent backgrounds become white.

    Can I convert many images at once?

    Yes. Select as many as you like and download them all as a ZIP file.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Your files are processed by your own browser and never leave your device. Nothing is sent to a server, so it works for confidential documents and you can even use it offline once the page has loaded.
