How to compress an image

  1. Choose one or more images.
  2. Pick JPG or WebP, a maximum width and a quality. 70% quality at 1920 px suits most uses.
  3. Press Compress and see the size saving for each image.
  4. Download each image or all of them as a ZIP.

How to get the smallest files

Two settings matter most. Reducing the width has the biggest effect: a 12-megapixel phone photo resized to 1920 px wide is around a quarter of the pixels. Lowering the quality to 60–75% removes detail you are unlikely to notice. WebP files are usually 25–35% smaller than JPG at the same quality.

Typical results

A 4 MB phone photo usually compresses to between 250 KB and 600 KB at 1920 px and 70% quality. Screenshots and graphics saved as PNG can shrink even more when converted, but use PNG-friendly settings if you need sharp text.

Use cases

Fit photos under email attachment limits, meet upload size limits on job and government forms, and speed up websites and online shops, where smaller images load faster and help search rankings.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reduce the size of a photo?

Choose the photo, keep the default settings and press Compress. Download the smaller copy.

How do I compress an image to under 100 KB?

Choose a maximum width of 800 px and a quality of around 60%. Check the size shown and lower the quality if needed.

Will compression reduce quality?

Slightly, but at 70% or higher most people can't see the difference on screen.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Your files are processed by your own browser and never leave your device. Nothing is sent to a server, so it works for confidential documents and you can even use it offline once the page has loaded.
