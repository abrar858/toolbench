    Electronic vs digital signatures

    An electronic signature is any signature added to a document electronically. A digital signature is one particular kind that uses a certificate and cryptography to show who signed and that nothing changed afterwards. Here's what each one does, and when a simple electronic signature is enough.

    • Updated 18 November 2026

    The short answer

    Electronic signatureDigital signature
    What it isA typed name, a drawn or uploaded signature image, a tick box or an “I agree” clickA cryptographic seal made with a private key and a certificate issued to the signer
    Shows who signedOnly through surrounding evidence, such as the email it came fromThe certificate links the signature to an identity, checked to the level the certificate provider promises
    Shows later changesNoYes: PDF readers flag the signature as invalid if the document changes after signing
    What you needAny e-signing tool, including Sign PDFA digital ID or a signing service that issues certificates
    CostUsually freeOften paid, through a certificate or signing-service provider

    Is an electronic signature valid in the UK?

    For most everyday documents, yes. In England and Wales the Law Commission confirmed in its 2019 report on the electronic execution of documents that an electronic signature is capable of validly signing a document, including a deed, as long as the person signing intends to authenticate it and any other formalities for that kind of document are met. The Electronic Communications Act 2000 lets electronic signatures be used as evidence, and under UK eIDAS a qualified electronic signature has the same legal effect as a handwritten one.

    Scotland and Northern Ireland have their own rules, and other countries differ, so check if the document is for use elsewhere.

    When a simple electronic signature may not be enough

    • Wills. In England and Wales a will must still be signed and witnessed in person. The temporary rule allowing video witnessing ended on 31 January 2024 (GOV.UK guidance).
    • Deeds that need a witness, such as some property documents. HM Land Registry only accepts electronic signatures that follow its practice guide 82, usually arranged through a conveyancer.
    • The recipient's own rules. Banks, courts, government departments and overseas organisations may insist on a handwritten signature or a particular kind of digital signature.

    If you're unsure, ask whoever you're sending it to which kind of signature they accept. This page is general information, not legal advice.

    What ToolsBravo's Sign PDF does

    Sign PDF adds a simple electronic signature: your drawn, typed or uploaded signature, plus your name, initials or the date, placed onto the pages of the PDF in your browser. The document isn't uploaded anywhere.

    It doesn't create a certificate-based digital signature and it doesn't check who you are. That suits tenancy and consent forms, school forms, quotes, job offers and similar documents, where the other side accepts electronic signatures.

    Making a signed PDF more trustworthy

    • Send it from your usual email address, so there's a record of who sent it and when.
    • Add the date next to your signature.
    • Keep a copy of exactly what you sent.
    • Remember that anyone with PDF-editing software can change a PDF signed with a picture of a signature. If the other side needs proof that nothing changed, ask them to send it through a service that adds a digital signature.

    How to tell if a PDF has a digital signature

    Open it in Adobe Acrobat Reader. A digitally signed PDF shows a banner such as “Signed and all signatures are valid” and lists each signature in the Signatures panel, with the certificate behind it. A signature that is only an image on the page doesn't appear there.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is a typed name a legal signature?

    In England and Wales it generally can be, for most everyday documents, if you type it intending to sign. Some documents, such as wills, have stricter rules.

    Can I sign a PDF without a digital ID?

    Yes. Draw, type or upload your signature with Sign PDF and download the signed copy. You only need a digital ID if the recipient asks for a certificate-based digital signature.

    Is ToolsBravo's signature a digital signature?

    No. It's a simple electronic signature placed on the page. It doesn't use a certificate or verify your identity.

    Do I need a witness?

    Only if the document itself requires one, for example a deed or a will. Check with whoever asked you to sign.
