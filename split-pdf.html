    Split PDF and extract pages

    Pull out the pages you need, split a PDF into several smaller files, or save every page as a separate PDF.

    • Free, no sign-up
    • Runs privately in your browser
    • Instant results
    ⇪Choose a PDF fileor drag and drop a file here · files stay on your device

    Choose a PDF to start.

    How to split a PDF

    1. Choose a PDF file.
    2. Pick how to split: extract chosen pages into one PDF, make one PDF for each range, or make one PDF for every page.
    3. Enter the pages, for example 1-3, 5, 8- (8- means page 8 to the end).
    4. Press Split PDF, then download each file or all of them as a ZIP.

    Writing page ranges

    • 5: just page 5.
    • 1-3: pages 1, 2 and 3.
    • 8-: page 8 to the last page.
    • 1-3, 5, 8-: several ranges separated by commas.

    With Make one PDF for each range, 1-3, 4-6 gives two files: pages 1–3 and pages 4–6.

    Quality and privacy

    Pages are copied without being re-rendered, so text stays sharp and searchable and nothing is compressed. The splitting happens on your device; your PDF is never uploaded.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I save one page of a PDF?

    Choose the PDF, keep 'Extract chosen pages into one PDF', type the page number and press Split PDF.

    Can I split a PDF into single pages?

    Yes. Choose 'Make one PDF for every page' and download them all as a ZIP file.

    Does splitting reduce quality?

    No. Pages are copied exactly, not converted.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Your files are processed by your own browser and never leave your device. Nothing is sent to a server, so it works for confidential documents and you can even use it offline once the page has loaded.
