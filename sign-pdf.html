    How to sign a PDF

    1. Choose the PDF you need to sign, or drag it onto the box.
    2. Press Signature and draw it with your mouse, finger or stylus, type your name, or upload a picture of your signature.
    3. Drag the signature to the right place and drag its corner to resize it. Add initials, your name, the date or other text the same way.
    4. Press Download signed PDF, check the placement, and save the file.

    Three ways to make your signature

    • Draw: sign in the box with a mouse, trackpad, finger or stylus. On a phone, turning it sideways gives you more room.
    • Type: type your name and it's written in a handwriting-style font. This is also the easiest option if you use a keyboard or screen reader.
    • Upload: photograph or scan your signature on white paper and upload it. Tick Make a white background transparent so only the ink appears on the page. A PNG with a transparent background works best.

    Signatures you make are remembered until you close or leave the page, so you can place the same signature on several pages without drawing it again.

    Signing on your phone

    Open this page in Chrome, Safari or another mobile browser and choose the PDF from your files, downloads or email attachments. Draw your signature with your finger, then drag it into place. Pinch-zooming the browser isn't needed: use the + and − buttons to zoom the document, and the page number box to jump between pages. The signed file is saved to your downloads, ready to attach to an email or upload to a form.

    Initials, names, dates and other text

    Many contracts and tenancy agreements ask you to initial every page. Make your initials once, then press Initials and pick them from Use one you made earlier for each page. Name and Text add typed text in a clear font, and Date fills in today's date in the format you choose. Double-click any text, or select it and press Enter, to change it. Every item can be moved, resized or removed, and Undo reverses a mistake.

    Private by design

    Many online signing services upload your document to their servers. Here, the PDF is opened, displayed and signed entirely by your own browser using the open-source pdf.js and pdf-lib libraries, which are hosted on this site. Your document and signature are never uploaded, never stored, and are cleared from the page when you press Start over or close the tab. That makes it suitable for contracts, payslips, medical forms and other confidential paperwork.

    What you download

    The signed file is your original PDF with your signatures and text added on top. Pages are not converted into images, so the original text stays sharp, selectable and searchable, and the file stays a similar size. Rotated pages and documents with a mix of page sizes, such as A4 and US Letter or portrait and landscape, are supported. Before downloading, the file is reopened and checked, so you don't get a broken or incomplete PDF. Signatures and text are written as ordinary PDF page content, not as comments or form layers, so they show in any standard PDF viewer, including Adobe Acrobat, web browsers and phone apps.

    Electronic signatures and their limits

    This tool adds an electronic signature: an image of your signature, or your typed name, placed on the document. In the UK and many other countries, electronic signatures like this are widely used and accepted for everyday paperwork such as letters, forms, tenancy agreements and many contracts.

    It is not a certified or cryptographic digital signature. It doesn't verify who signed, doesn't add a certificate, and doesn't lock the document against later changes. Some documents have stricter rules: wills, for example, generally still need a handwritten signature and witnesses, and some organisations require a particular signing service. If you're unsure whether an electronic signature is acceptable for a document, check with the person or organisation that asked for it, or take legal advice.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I sign a PDF for free?

    Choose the PDF here, press Signature, draw or type your signature, drag it into place and press Download signed PDF. There's no account, watermark or limit.

    Can I sign a PDF on my iPhone or Android phone?

    Yes. Open this page in your phone's browser, choose the PDF, draw your signature with your finger and download the signed file.

    Is it safe to sign confidential documents here?

    Yes. The document is processed by your own browser and never uploaded or stored. Close the page or press Start over when you're done.

    Can I add my signature to several pages?

    Yes. Make your signature once, then press Signature again on each page and choose it from the signatures you made earlier.

    Can I sign a password-protected PDF?

    Not directly. Open it with its password in your PDF reader, save or print a copy without a password, then sign that copy.

    Is this a legally binding signature?

    Electronic signatures are widely accepted for everyday documents, but this isn't a certified digital signature and doesn't verify identity. Some documents, such as wills, have stricter requirements. Check with whoever asked you to sign if you're unsure.
