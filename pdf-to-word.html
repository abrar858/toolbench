How to convert PDF to Word

  1. Choose a PDF file.
  2. Press Convert to Word. The text is read page by page.
  3. Check the extracted text, then download the .docx file or copy the text.

What gets converted

The converter keeps the text and rebuilds paragraphs, with a page break between each page of the PDF. It does not copy images, tables, columns, fonts or exact layout. That makes it ideal when you need to edit or reuse the words in a document, such as a letter, report or contract, rather than reproduce its design.

Scanned PDFs

A scanned PDF is a picture of a page with no real text inside, so there is nothing to extract. If the converter finds no text, the PDF needs OCR (optical character recognition) first. Many phone scanner apps and Microsoft Word itself can do this.

Why it's private

Many PDF to Word converters upload your document to a server. Here the text is read with pdf.js and the Word file is built in your browser, so confidential documents never leave your device.

Frequently asked questions

Can I edit a PDF in Word?

Yes. Convert it to .docx here, then open the file in Word, edit it and save it again as a PDF if you need to.

Will the formatting be the same as the PDF?

No. Text and paragraphs are kept, but images, tables and exact layout are not. For a perfect visual copy, use PDF to JPG instead.

Does it work with Google Docs?

Yes. Upload the .docx file to Google Drive and open it with Google Docs.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Your files are processed by your own browser and never leave your device. Nothing is sent to a server, so it works for confidential documents and you can even use it offline once the page has loaded.
