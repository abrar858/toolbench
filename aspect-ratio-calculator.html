    Aspect ratio calculator

    Find the aspect ratio of an image or video and work out a new width or height that keeps exactly the same shape.

    • Free, no sign-up
    • Runs privately in your browser
    • Instant results

    Enter positive whole pixels up to 1,000,000. The other dimension is rounded to the nearest pixel. This calculates dimensions; it does not upload or resize an image file.

    How to use the aspect ratio calculator

    1. Choose a preset such as Full HD or 4K, or type the original width and height in pixels.
    2. Pick whether you want to set a new width or a new height.
    3. Enter the new size and read the matching dimension and the simplified ratio.

    What is an aspect ratio?

    An aspect ratio is the relationship between width and height, written as width:height. A 1920 × 1080 video and a 1280 × 720 video are both 16:9, so one can be scaled to the other without stretching. Common ratios include 16:9 for widescreen video, 9:16 for vertical stories and reels, 4:3 for older screens and many tablets, and 1:1 for square social posts.

    How to resize without distortion

    Keep the ratio fixed and change one side. New height = new width × original height ÷ original width. For example, resizing 1920 × 1080 to a width of 1280 gives a height of 1280 × 1080 ÷ 1920 = 720 pixels.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the aspect ratio of 1920 × 1080?

    1920 × 1080 is 16:9, the standard widescreen ratio for Full HD video.

    What size is a 9:16 vertical video?

    A common size is 1080 × 1920 pixels, used for phone-first video formats.

    Why does the result have a decimal?

    Some sizes do not divide evenly. Round to the nearest whole pixel, or pick a size that is a multiple of the ratio to avoid rounding.
