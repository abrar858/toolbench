    Privacy

    ToolsBravo is designed so the calculations, conversions and text processing in its tools happen in your browser.

    The site does not use accounts, an application database, analytics scripts or advertising trackers.

    External services

    Fonts and code libraries are served from toolsbravo.com itself, so using the tools doesn't connect your browser to Google or other third parties. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process technical connection data such as IP addresses and server logs under its policies.

    Information you enter

    Tool inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to a ToolsBravo application server. Avoid entering sensitive information on shared devices. Browser extensions and the device you use may have their own access.

    Cookies and local storage

    ToolsBravo does not set tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage is used only to remember your light or dark theme choice and, in the automation builder, your saved workflow. Both stay on your device. If advertising, analytics, accounts or other third-party services are introduced, this notice will be updated before launch.

    Contact

    For enquiries, use the project's GitHub repository. Do not post personal information in public issues.
