    Debt payoff calculator

    Find out how long it will take to clear a credit card or loan with a fixed monthly payment, how much interest you will pay, and what paying a little more saves.

    • Free, no sign-up
    • Runs privately in your browser
    • Updated for 2026/27

    By Bisma Zainab, developer of ToolsBravo · Figures checked against official sources · Reviewed 22 November 2026

    How to use the debt payoff calculator

    1. Enter the balance you owe.
    2. Enter the interest rate (APR).
    3. Enter the monthly payment you can afford.
    4. Read your debt-free date, the total interest and the saving from paying £50 more a month.

    Worked example

    A £3,000 credit card balance at 24.9% APR with payments of £150 a month takes 27 months to clear and costs about £915.95 in interest. Paying £200 a month clears it in 19 months and cuts the interest to about £631.43, a saving of £284.52.

    Why minimum payments take so long

    Each month, interest is added before your payment reduces the balance. If your payment barely covers the interest, most of it never touches what you owe. The calculator warns you if your payment does not cover the first month's interest.

    Getting help

    If you are struggling with debt, free and confidential advice is available from services such as MoneyHelper, StepChange and Citizens Advice.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long will it take to pay off my credit card?

    Enter your balance, APR and monthly payment and the calculator shows the number of months and an estimated debt-free date.

    Does it include new spending?

    No. It assumes no new spending, fees or rate changes while you pay the debt off.

    Should I pay off debt or save?

    High-interest debt usually costs more than savings earn, but keeping an emergency fund matters too. Consider free debt advice for your situation.
