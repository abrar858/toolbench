    How to edit a PDF

    1. Choose the PDF you want to edit, or drag it onto the box.
    2. To change existing text, press Edit text and click the line. Type the new wording and press Replace text.
    3. To remove something, drag over it with Whiteout, or with Redact for anything confidential.
    4. Add text, pictures, drawings, highlights or boxes with the other tools, and fill in any form fields.
    5. Press Download edited PDF.

    Change existing text

    Press Edit text and every line of text on the page is outlined. Click one, type the new wording and press Replace text. The tool covers the old line with the page's background colour and writes your text on the same baseline, in the closest standard font (sans, serif or monospaced), at the same size and colour, and bold if the original was. Leave the box empty and choose Remove the line instead to delete a line.

    It can't reuse the exact font a PDF was made with, because most PDFs include only the letters they already use. For matching text, the closest standard font at the same size usually looks right; for anything that must be pixel-perfect, edit the original Word or Google Docs file and save it as a PDF again.

    Whiteout or redaction?

    Whiteout covers part of a page with a box in any colour. It's quick and good for hiding mistakes, old dates or a logo, but the content underneath is still in the file, and anyone can copy it out.

    Redact is for anything confidential: account numbers, addresses, medical or legal details. On download, each page with a redaction is redrawn as an image with the black boxes burned in, so the hidden text is gone, not just covered. The file is also rebuilt without bookmarks or document properties, which can contain names and titles. The trade-off is that text on redacted pages can no longer be selected or searched.

    Badly redacted documents are a common way confidential information leaks: a black box drawn over selectable text can simply be copied out. Check any redacted file by trying to select the text under the boxes.

    Add text, pictures and drawings

    • Add text: click where it should go. Choose the font, size, colour and bold. Several lines are fine.
    • Image: add a logo, stamp or photo, then drag it into place and resize it from the corner.
    • Draw: draw freehand with a mouse, finger or stylus, in any colour and three thicknesses.
    • Highlight and Box: drag over text to highlight it, or draw a rectangle around something.

    Everything can be moved, resized and removed until you download. Undo and redo work for every change. To sign, use Sign PDF, which has a signature pad.

    Fill in PDF forms

    If the PDF has form fields, they show up as boxes you can type into, tick or choose from. Your answers are saved into the form itself, so they stay editable in other PDF readers. Tick Make answers permanent to flatten the form so the answers can't be changed. Forms made with Adobe's older XFA format aren't supported.

    Scanned PDFs

    A scanned page is a picture, so there's no text to click on. You can still edit it: cover words with Whiteout in the paper's colour, add new text on top, and redact anything confidential.

    Private, with no limits

    Many online PDF editors upload your file to their servers and delete it an hour or two later, and some limit how many tasks or pages you can do for free. Here the PDF is opened and changed by your own browser, so it never leaves your device, and there's no account, watermark or daily limit.

    What it can't do

    • Reflow paragraphs: changing a line doesn't move the lines below it. Shorter or slightly longer wording fits best.
    • Reuse the PDF's own embedded font.
    • Open password-protected PDFs. Remove the password in your PDF reader first.
    • Rearrange pages: use Delete PDF pages, Rotate PDF or Split PDF.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I edit text in a PDF for free?

    Choose the PDF, press Edit text, click the line you want to change, type the new wording and press Replace text. Then download the edited PDF.

    Can I edit a PDF with the same font?

    The tool matches the size, colour, weight and style (sans, serif or monospaced) of the original line using standard PDF fonts. It can't reuse the exact embedded font, so for a perfect match edit the original document.

    How do I remove text from a PDF?

    Use Edit text and choose Remove the line instead, or drag over it with Whiteout. For confidential information use Redact, which removes it permanently.

    Is whiteout the same as redaction?

    No. Whiteout only covers content; it can still be copied out of the file. Redact removes it: pages with redactions are rebuilt as images with the content gone.

    Can I fill in a PDF form?

    Yes. Form fields appear as boxes you can type into or tick. You can keep the form editable or flatten it so the answers are permanent.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Your files are processed by your own browser and never leave your device. Nothing is sent to a server, so it works for confidential documents and you can even use it offline once the page has loaded.
