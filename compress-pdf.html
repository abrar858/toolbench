    Compress PDF

    Shrink large scanned or photo-heavy PDFs so they fit email attachments and upload limits, or set a limit such as under 200 KB and let the tool find the best quality that fits.

    • Free, no sign-up
    • Runs privately in your browser
    • Instant results
    ⇪Choose a PDF fileor drag and drop a file here · files stay on your device

    Finds the best quality that fits under the limit, for upload forms with a maximum file size. Very long documents may not fit under the smallest limits.

    Pages are saved as images, so text in the compressed PDF can't be selected or searched. Best for scans and photo-heavy documents.

    Choose a PDF to start.

    How to compress a PDF

    1. Choose a PDF file. Its current size is shown.
    2. Pick Strong, Balanced or Light compression, or choose a Size limit such as Under 200 KB.
    3. Press Compress PDF and compare the before and after size, then download.

    How it shrinks files

    Each page is redrawn as a compressed JPEG image at the resolution you choose, then put back into a new PDF of the same page size. Scans and photos taken with a phone are often far larger than they need to be, and this can cut them by 50 to 90%.

    When not to use it

    Because pages become images, text in the compressed PDF can't be selected, copied or searched, and documents that are mostly text may get bigger, not smaller. If that happens the tool tells you and you should keep the original. Use Light compression for documents with small print.

    Which level?

    • Strong (72 dpi): smallest files, fine for reading on screen.
    • Balanced (110 dpi): a good default for email.
    • Light (150 dpi): sharper text, suitable for printing.

    Compress a PDF to 200 KB, 500 KB or 1 MB

    Job, university, visa and government upload forms often reject PDFs over a limit such as 200 KB, 500 KB or 1 MB. Choose Size limit, pick the limit and press Compress PDF. The tool tries settings from sharpest to smallest and keeps the best quality that fits, so you don't have to guess.

    How small a PDF can go depends mostly on how many pages it has, because every page becomes an image that needs some space. In our tests, a 4-page scan of 4.2 MB came out at 156 KB with the 200 KB limit, and a 10-page scan of 10.4 MB came out at 389 KB with the 500 KB limit. The same 4 pages couldn't get below 121 KB, so the 100 KB limit wasn't reachable, and the tool says so instead of handing you a file that's still too big.

    If it still won't fit

    • Leave out pages the form doesn't need with Delete PDF pages, or send the document in parts with Split PDF.
    • Rescan in greyscale at 150 dpi instead of colour at 300 dpi: the file starts much smaller.
    • If the PDF was made from photos, shrink the photos first with the image compressor, then join them with JPG to PDF.

    Why some PDFs are so big

    The size of a PDF depends mostly on its images. A page scanned at 300 or 600 dpi, or a phone photo turned into a PDF, can be several megabytes on its own, while a page of plain text is usually only a few kilobytes. That's why scans and photo-heavy documents shrink dramatically here, and text-only documents don't.

    Common size limits

    • Gmail attachments: up to 25 MB.
    • Outlook.com attachments: around 20 MB.
    • Job, university and government upload forms: often between 2 MB and 10 MB, and sometimes as low as 1 MB. Check the form's own limit.

    If you have a 15 MB scanned document and a 5 MB limit, choose Size limit: Under 5 MB and the tool finds the best quality that fits. If even the smallest setting is too big, split the document with Split PDF and send it in parts.

    Better options for text documents

    If your PDF is mostly text and came from Word, Google Docs or another app, the smallest file usually comes from exporting it again from that app: in Word, use File → Save as → PDF and choose Minimum size. On a Mac, Preview's Export → Quartz Filter → Reduce File Size also works, though it can reduce image quality a lot.

    If something goes wrong

    • The file got bigger: the tool tells you and doesn't offer the bigger file. Keep the original.
    • I can't select or search the text: that's expected, because pages are saved as images. Keep the original for anything that needs searchable text.
    • Small print is blurry: use Light compression.
    • Signatures or form fields disappeared: they become part of the page image. Sign and fill in the original instead.

    Step by step: shrink a scan for email

    1. Choose the PDF and note its size.
    2. Pick Balanced and press Compress PDF.
    3. Check the new size against the limit, and open the file to make sure the text is still readable.
    4. If it's still too big, run the original again on Strong.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I make a PDF smaller to email?

    Choose the PDF, select Balanced and press Compress PDF. Most scanned documents shrink enough to attach.

    How do I compress a PDF to under 200 KB?

    Choose the PDF, set Size limit to Under 200 KB and press Compress PDF. It picks the best quality that fits, and tells you if the PDF can't get that small, for example because it has many pages.

    Why can't I select text after compressing?

    Pages are saved as images to make them smaller. Keep the original if you need selectable text.

    Will it work on a PDF with a password?

    No. Remove the password first.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Your files are processed by your own browser and never leave your device. Nothing is sent to a server, so it works for confidential documents and you can even use it offline once the page has loaded.

    How do I compress a PDF to under 1 MB?

    Set Size limit to Under 1 MB and press Compress PDF. For long scans that still can't fit, split the PDF into smaller parts and compress each one.
