Age calculator

Find an exact age in years, months and days from a date of birth, and see how long it is until the next birthday.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Runs privately in your browser
  • Instant results

How to use the age calculator

  1. Enter the date of birth.
  2. Leave 'Age on this date' as today, or choose another date to find someone's age then.
  3. Read the age in years, months and days and the countdown to the next birthday.

Uses for an exact age

Check eligibility dates for school starting age, driving, pensions or insurance, fill in forms that ask for age on a specific date, or work out how old someone was at a past event. The calculation uses real calendar months, so it handles months of different lengths and leap years.

Birthdays on 29 February

People born on 29 February only have a calendar birthday in leap years. In other years the calculator still counts complete years of age correctly.

How exact age is worked out

The calculator counts complete years first, then complete months, then the remaining days, using real calendar dates. For example, someone born on 15 March 1990 is 36 years, 8 months and 0 days old on 15 November 2026, and their next birthday is in 120 days.

Because months have different lengths, an age in months and days can look uneven around the end of a month. That is normal and matches how ages are counted on official forms.

You can also use it to find someone's age on a past or future date, for example their age at the start of a school year or on a pension or insurance start date.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my age on a specific date?

Enter your date of birth and change 'Age on this date' to the date you need.

How many days old am I?

Use the date difference calculator with your date of birth and today's date to get the total number of days.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is saved.
