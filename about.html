About Toolbench

Toolbench provides free everyday calculators, text utilities, converters and developer tools. It is built as a lightweight website that works on phones and computers without an account.

How it works

Most operations run directly in your browser. There is no paid subscription or sign-up required to use the tools.

Financial calculators

Finance results are estimates, not financial, tax or legal advice. UK pay estimates use stated assumptions and may not reflect your tax code, region, benefits, pension scheme or other deductions. Confirm important figures with official guidance or a qualified professional.
