    About ToolsBravo

    Free everyday calculators, text tools, converters and developer utilities that work on phones and computers, with no account, subscription or app to install.

    Private by design

    The tools run in your browser. What you type into a calculator, text box or converter is processed on your own device and is not uploaded to a ToolsBravo server. The site has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking cookies. Fonts and libraries are hosted on ToolsBravo itself, so your browser doesn't contact other companies to use the tools. Read the privacy notice for details.

    How results are checked

    The source code is public on GitHub. Automated tests run on every change. They check the UK income tax and National Insurance figures against worked examples, test that every tool loads without errors, and confirm that worked examples quoted on each page match what the tool calculates.

    UK finance tools use the published rates for the stated tax year. Each finance page shows the date its rates were last checked against GOV.UK guidance. Rates are reviewed each spring before the new tax year starts on 6 April.

    Limits of the calculators

    Finance results are estimates for guidance, not financial, tax or legal advice. UK pay estimates use stated assumptions and may not reflect your tax code, region, benefits, pension scheme, student loan or other deductions. Confirm important figures with official guidance, your payslip or a qualified professional. Health tools such as the BMI calculator are screening estimates, not a diagnosis.

    Report a problem or suggest a tool

    Found a wrong result, a broken page or a missing tool? Open an issue in the ToolsBravo GitHub repository. Please do not include personal information in public issues.
